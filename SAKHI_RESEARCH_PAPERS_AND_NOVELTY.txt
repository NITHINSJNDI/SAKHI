PART 1: PCOS (POLYCYSTIC OVARY SYNDROME) SCREENING PAPERS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PAPER TITLE:
   "Automated Screening of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome Using Machine Learning Techniques"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: P. Mehrotra, J. Chatterjee, C. Sharma, A. Khilnani
• Journal & Year: Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine, Vol. 197, 2021.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Mehrotra et al. developed classification models (SVM, Random Forest, Naive Bayes) 
    using a clinical dataset of 40 clinical and metabolic parameters.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Their model strictly depends on invasive laboratory blood 
    biomarkers (LH/FSH ratio, serum testosterone, fasting insulin) and pelvic 
    ultrasound follicle counts. This model CANNOT be used at home by an everyday woman 
    who has not already visited an expensive tertiary care hospital.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - ZERO-COST, NON-INVASIVE SCREENING: Sakhi eliminates the prerequisite for hospital 
    ultrasound folliculometry for initial triage.
  - SYMPTOM-TIME CORRELATION: Sakhi correlates self-reported cycle regularity, 
    bleeding duration, hyperandrogenism physical markers (acne, hirsutism, hair thinning), 
    and cramp severity logged during everyday life.
  - REAL-WORLD ACCESSIBILITY: Sakhi empowers women in rural, remote, or low-income 
    settings to detect early risk before spending money on hospital tests.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PAPER TITLE:
   "Prediction of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) Using Machine Learning Techniques"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: A. Denny, A. Raj, A. Ashok, C. M. Ram, R. George
• Conference & Year: IEEE International Conference on Smart Systems and Inventive Technology (ICSSIT), 2019.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Built supervised ML classifiers that achieved ~89% diagnostic accuracy on 
    hospitalized patient questionnaires.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: The model acts as a "black box" that outputs only a final binary 
    label ("PCOS: Yes/No"). It provides zero interpretability to the patient or doctor 
    explaining WHICH specific factors elevated her risk.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - EXPLAINABLE AI (XAI) BREAKDOWN: Sakhi translates complex ML outputs into a 
    transparent clinical factor breakdown.
  - TRANSPARENT INDICATORS: Sakhi tells the woman exactly why her score changed 
    (e.g., "+25% risk due to bleeding duration >7 days", "-4% due to healthy cycle length").
  - PATIENT EMPOWERMENT: Instead of creating panic with a blind probability, Sakhi 
    gives the woman clear talking points to discuss with her gynecologist.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PAPER TITLE:
   "Diagnosis of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome Using Machine Learning Algorithms"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: S. Bharati, P. Podder, M. R. H. Mondal
• Journal & Year: IEEE Access, Vol. 8, pp. 206411–206422, 2020.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Applied CatBoost and XGBoost on static, one-time cross-sectional clinical data.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Ignores the dynamic, longitudinal nature of female cycles. A woman's 
    cycle fluctuates over months due to stress, travel, or lifestyle changes. A single 
    static assessment produces frequent false positives.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - CONTINUOUS LONGITUDINAL MONITORING: Sakhi tracks cycle parameters across consecutive 
    months via 60-second daily check-ins.
  - DYNAMIC RE-CALIBRATION: Sakhi updates its risk assessment dynamically as new cycle 
    histories are recorded, distinguishing true chronic hormonal dysfunction from 
    temporary, stress-induced cycle delays.

PART 2: PREECLAMPSIA & MATERNAL CARDIOVASCULAR HEALTH PAPERS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PAPER TITLE:
   "The International Federation of Gynecology and Obstetrics (FIGO) Initiative on 
    Pre-eclampsia: A Pragmatic Guide for First-Trimester Screening and Prevention"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: L. C. Poon, A. Shennan, J. A. Hyett, et al.
• Journal & Year: International Journal of Gynecology & Obstetrics, Vol. 145(S1), pp. 1–33, 2019.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Developed the world's clinical gold-standard first-trimester screening algorithm.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Relies on Uterine Artery Pulsatility Index (UtAPI measured via 
    transvaginal Doppler ultrasound) and serum Placental Growth Factor (PlGF) blood assays. 
    Millions of pregnant women in developing regions cannot access Doppler machines or 
    specialized biochemical analyzers during their early trimesters.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - AT-HOME CLINICAL TRIAGE: Sakhi takes FIGO's core epidemiological risk criteria 
    (maternal hypertension history, gestational age) and pairs them with accessible, 
    daily at-home blood pressure telemetry (systolic and diastolic readings).
  - PROACTIVE ALERTING: Rather than waiting for a monthly hospital visit, Sakhi 
    identifies emerging hypertensive trends day-by-day in the mother's home environment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PAPER TITLE:
   "Competing Risks Model in Screening for Preeclampsia by Maternal Characteristics 
    and Medical History"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: D. Wright, M. Y. Tan, N. O'Gorman, L. C. Poon, A. Syngelaki, K. H. Nicolaides
• Journal & Year: American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Vol. 223(5), pp. 742.e1–742.e15, 2020.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Evaluated survival-time distributions for preeclampsia delivery risk using maternal 
    history recorded during clinical registration.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Treats maternal history as a static mathematical baseline, failing 
    to interactively communicate with the mother or monitor daily acute symptoms.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - INTERACTIVE LIFE-DATA INTEGRATION: Sakhi integrates baseline maternal hypertension 
    history recorded during onboarding directly with daily vital logs and acute symptoms.
  - TRISYMPTOM TRIAGE: Correlates high blood pressure with maternal-reported symptoms 
    (severe frontal headache, vision blur, peripheral edema), triggering emergency triage 
    if a dangerous preeclamptic triad manifests.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PAPER TITLE:
   "Early Prediction of Preeclampsia via Machine Learning"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: I. Marić, A. Tsur, N. Aghaeepour, A. Montanari, D. K. Stevenson, V. D. Winn
• Journal & Year: American Journal of Obstetrics & Gynecology MFM, Vol. 2(2), 100100, 2020.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Applied machine learning to retrospective Electronic Health Records (EHR) of 
    pregnant patients, finding blood pressure to be the single strongest longitudinal signal.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Retrospective study performed on hospital databases after patient 
    discharge. Did not provide real-time patient-facing tools or bedside warning systems.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - LIVE REAL-TIME TELEMETRY: Sakhi turns Marić et al.'s finding into an active real-time 
    patient guardian. The second a pregnant mother logs BP exceeding 140/90 mmHg, Sakhi's 
    engine flags the trend and alerts the mother immediately.

PART 3: GESTATIONAL DIABETES MELLITUS (GDM) PAPERS

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PAPER TITLE:
   "Prediction of Gestational Diabetes Based on Nationwide Electronic Health Records"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: N. S. Artzi, S. Shilo, E. Hadar, H. Rossman, S. Barbash-Hazan, A. Ben-Haroush, 
           R. D. Balicer, B. Feldman, E. Segal
• Journal & Year: Nature Medicine, Vol. 26(1), pp. 71–76, 2020.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Used Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDT) on 547,000 pregnancies in Israel to predict 
    GDM risk before the standard 24–28 week diagnosis window.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Designed for backend hospital healthcare system analytics, not for 
    direct patient engagement, home glucose tracking, or daily lifestyle counseling.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - PATIENT-CENTRIC INTEGRATION: Sakhi bridges predictive risk into daily maternal hands. 
    Mothers log fasting blood sugar and immediately see their score against clinical 
    fasting targets (<95 mg/dL).
  - BALANCED NUTRITION TRACKING: Sakhi pairs sugar monitoring with proactive daily 
    nutrition tracking (Protein, Iron, Calcium, Hydration) to help prevent insulin spikes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. PAPER TITLE:
   "Machine Learning for Predicting Gestational Diabetes Mellitus from Early Pregnancy 
    Electronic Health Records"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: Y. T. Wu, C. J. Zhang, B. W. Mol, A. Kawai, C. Li, L. Chen, J. Z. Sheng, et al.
• Journal & Year: Frontiers in Endocrinology, Vol. 12, 696500, 2021.
• Prior Research Focus & Limitation:
  - Applied ML to hospital clinical databases to classify early GDM risk.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Does not offer multi-condition correlation (e.g., assessing how GDM 
    interacts with maternal hypertension or pre-existing PCOS history).
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - ENSEMBLE COMORBIDITY CORRELATION: Sakhi evaluates how PCOS history from "Her Mode" 
    predisposes a mother to higher GDM risk in "Mom Mode", providing unified comorbidity 
    tracking that prior studies evaluated in isolation.

9. COMMERCIAL PATENT:
   "System and Method for Predicting a Phase of a Menstrual Cycle"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Patent Number: US Patent 10,860,797 B2 (Granted Dec 8, 2020)
• Assignee & Inventors: Flo Health, Inc. (R. Gurski, et al.)
• Prior Art Scope & Limitation:
  - Patents neural network techniques for predicting menstrual phases and fertility windows 
    based on historical cycle timestamps.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: The patent is strictly confined to an isolated period tracker. When a 
    user conceives, the app switches to an advertising-heavy pregnancy tracking interface 
    without clinical risk prediction (PCOS, Thyroid, Preeclampsia, GDM).
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - DUAL-MODE LIFECYCLE CONTINUUM: Sakhi introduces an integrated dual-state engine 
    ("Her Mode" for cycle & hormonal balance; "Mom Mode" for gestational care).
  - PRESERVATION OF CLINICAL BASELINES: Rather than discarding menstrual data, Sakhi 
    uses pre-pregnancy cycle telemetry to calibrate gestational risk models.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. COMMERCIAL PATENT:
    "Systems and Methods for Dynamic Menstrual Cycle and Ovulation Window Prediction"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Patent Number: US Patent 11,380,443 B2 (Granted July 5, 2022)
• Assignee: BioWink GmbH (Clue App)
• Prior Art Scope & Limitation:
  - Patents statistical distribution models to adjust calendar dates for ovulation windows.
  - MAJOR LIMITATION: Purely calendar and date-centric. Does not provide conversational 
    voice interaction, visual doll lip-sync, or clinical comorbidity explainability.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - CONVERSATIONAL AI DOLL AVATAR: Sakhi combines cycle modeling with an interactive, 
    lip-synced voice companion that answers personal questions aloud.
  - CLINICAL EXPLAINABILITY & DOCTOR PDF: Provides clear factor breakdowns and generates 
    1-click doctor summary reports for clinical appointments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. PAPER TITLE:
    "Real-World Menstrual Cycle Characteristics of More Than 600,000 Menstrual Cycles"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Authors: J. R. Bull, S. P. Rowland, E. B. Scherwitzl, R. Scherwitzl, K. G. Danielsson, J. Harper
• Journal & Year: npj Digital Medicine (Nature Partner Journals), Vol. 2(1), 83, 2019.
• Prior Research Focus & Key Finding:
  - Analyzed over 600,000 cycles across 124,648 women, scientifically proving that ONLY 13% 
    of women have a standard 28-day cycle. Follicular and luteal phase variations are vast.
• SAKHI'S NOVELTY & ADVANTAGE:
  - MATHEMATICAL ADAPTATION: Sakhi directly implements Bull et al.'s findings by discarding 
    rigid 28-day templates in favor of dynamic moving-average phase intervals.
  - ADAPTIVE OVULATION CALENDAR: Sakhi accurately predicts ovulation even for women whose 
    cycles naturally span 21–45 days.
