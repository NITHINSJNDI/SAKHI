1. WHAT IS SAKHI?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sakhi (meaning "a close female friend" in Sanskrit and Hindi) is an easy-to-use,
friendly, and intelligent health platform created for women. 

In everyday life, women often navigate menstruation, hormonal imbalances, 
fertility questions, pregnancy, and emotional changes in silence, without quick
access to clear health guidance. Sakhi acts as a 24/7 personal health guide that
listens to women, tracks their physical and emotional symptoms, and provides 
compassionate, easy-to-understand health insights.

Sakhi is designed with two distinct modes to support a woman throughout her life:
  • "Her Mode"  - For menstrual cycle tracking, hormone wellness, ovulation, and
                  early screening for conditions like PCOS and Thyroid problems.
  • "Mom Mode"  - For pregnant mothers to track baby growth, fetal movements,
                  blood pressure, blood sugar, and safe pregnancy milestones.


2. HOW IS THIS USEFUL TO WOMEN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Personalized to Her Real Body:
   Most apps assume every woman has a textbook 28-day cycle. Sakhi learns her 
   actual body rhythm, adapting to irregular periods, stress variations, and 
   postpartum return.

2. Early Health Warnings (PCOS & Thyroid Screening):
   Many women suffer from PCOS or Thyroid imbalances for years before getting 
   diagnosed. By simply logging everyday signs (bleeding length, cramps, acne, 
   mood, and cycle intervals), Sakhi warns her early if risk factors appear, 
   empowering her to visit a doctor before conditions worsen.

3. Safer Pregnancies & Baby Monitoring:
   High blood pressure (Preeclampsia) and high blood sugar (Gestational Diabetes) 
   can endanger both mother and child during pregnancy. Sakhi monitors these vital 
   signs daily, helping mothers notice red flags early.

4. 24/7 Voice & Chat Companion:
   Women can talk or type directly to Sakhi in plain, comfortable language. Sakhi 
   replies with warmth, guidance, and medical reassurance, breaking the isolation 
   and fear around sensitive health issues.

5. Doctor-Ready Medical Reports:
   Women often forget their exact symptoms or dates when visiting their gynecologist. 
   Sakhi generates a clean 1-click summary report that she can print or show to her 
   doctor during clinic visits.

6. A Lifelong Companion:
   When a woman becomes pregnant, she doesn't need a new app. With one click, she 
   switches from cycle tracking to pregnancy care, and back again postpartum.


3. THE TWO CORE JOURNEYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. "HER MODE" (Cycle & Hormonal Intelligence)
---------------------------------------------
  • Smart Cycle & Bleeding Tracking:
    Predicts when her next period will arrive and how long bleeding will last.
  • Ovulation & Fertile Window Calendar:
    Calculates her most fertile days to help her plan conception or understand 
    her natural cycle rhythms.
  • Hormone Wellness & Risk Indicators:
    Evaluates risk for PCOS and Thyroid imbalances by analyzing irregular cycle 
    patterns, cramps severity, heavy bleeding, and related physical symptoms.
  • Daily Check-in & Emotional Wellness:
    A 60-second daily check to log energy, sleep hours, physical pain, and mood 
    (happy, calm, anxious, fatigued), showing how her hormones influence feelings.
  • Animated Live Health Visuals:
    Clear graphs showing cycle history, pain trends, and sleep over time.

B. "MOM MODE" (Pregnancy & Maternal Care)
----------------------------------------
  • Weekly Baby Growth & Trimester Milestones:
    Visualizes baby development week-by-week using friendly fruit comparisons 
    (from a tiny blueberry in early weeks up to a full pumpkin at term).
  • Fetal Kick Counter:
    Tracks baby movement across morning, afternoon, and night so the mother can 
    confirm her baby is active and healthy.
  • Vital Signs Screening:
    Monitors blood pressure and fasting blood sugar, watching for signs of 
    preeclampsia and gestational diabetes.
  • Daily Nutrition Guide:
    Reminds mothers about essential pregnancy nutrients: Protein, Iron, Calcium, 
    and daily hydration.
  • Prenatal Visit Schedule:
    Organizes routine antenatal checkup reminders and medical scans.
  • Emergency Red-Flag Triage:
    Immediately alerts the mother if dangerous symptoms (such as severe swelling, 
    blurry vision, or sudden bleeding) are reported, urging her to visit an 
    OB/GYN or hospital without delay.


4. END-TO-END WORKFLOW (HOW IT WORKS STEP-BY-STEP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Simple Sign-In
  • The user visits the website and signs in securely with one tap using her 
    Google account.

Step 2: Friendly 2-Minute Onboarding
  • Sakhi welcomes her by name and asks: "Which journey would you like to set up?"
  • If she chooses "Her Mode":
    - Sakhi asks for the date her last period started, typical cycle length, and 
      any known past health history.
  • If she chooses "Mom Mode":
    - Sakhi asks how many weeks pregnant she is, her due date, and whether this is 
      her first pregnancy.

Step 3: Talking With the Sakhi AI Avatar
  • On her private dashboard, an interactive animated Sakhi doll greets her.
  • She can speak aloud using her microphone or type questions like:
    "Why are my cramps so bad this month?" or 
    "Is it normal to feel nausea in week 12?"
  • Sakhi answers aloud with a caring voice and offers supportive, reliable health 
    guidance.

Step 4: 60-Second Daily Check-in
  • Once every day, Sakhi prompts her for a quick check-in:
    - In Her Mode: Bleeding flow, cramp level, symptoms, sleep, and emotional mood.
    - In Mom Mode: Blood pressure, sugar reading, baby kicks, symptoms, and sleep.
  • Filling this in takes less than a minute.

Step 5: Automatic Dashboard Update
  • Immediately after the check-in, all charts and health risk gauges animate and 
    update with her latest numbers.
  • She can visually spot trends, such as how sleep affects her mood or whether her 
    blood pressure is remaining stable.

Step 6: Smart Alerts & Clinical Help
  • If her numbers look healthy, Sakhi provides positive encouragement.
  • If any warning sign or risk emerges (such as elevated blood pressure or severe 
    pain), Sakhi explains what it means in simple words and advises her to speak 
    with a doctor.

Step 7: Seamless Life Stage Switching
  • If a woman in Her Mode becomes pregnant, she opens Settings and taps "Switch to 
    Mom Mode." Her dashboard transforms immediately into pregnancy care without 
    losing her personal account history.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Interactive Health Charts: Recharts
  • Conversational AI Companion: Google Gemini AI

  • Database & Cloud Storage: PostgreSQL (Supabase)

  • Voice & Speech Integration: Web Speech API (Speech Recognition & Speech Synthesis)
